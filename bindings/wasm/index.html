Paste a DIMACS CNF, open or drop a file, or pick an example. tididi-bonsai compiles the formula into a Tree Decision Diagram and counts its models: the model count, the weighted count, or either one projected onto the formula's show variables. The weights and the show variables come from the formula's own c p weight and c p show lines, and its c t line names the task unless you pick one. Everything runs in this tab and nothing is uploaded. tididi-bonsai is also a Rust crate with a command-line tool and API documentation; its input reference describes the format, and its evaluation compares it with other exact counters.

tididi-bonsai compiles a formula bottom-up over a
    vtree, a binary tree with one variable at each leaf, into a Tree
    Decision Diagram. Each clause waits at the lowest vtree node whose subtree
    holds all its variables. At each node, the diagrams of its two sides are
    joined, and the clauses waiting there are conjoined in. A diagram node is
    the disjunction of its pairs, and a pair, drawn as a box of two halves, is
    the conjunction of a function of the variables on the left and one of
    those on the right; ⊤ stands for a side that every assignment satisfies.
    Beside each node is the number of assignments to its variables that
    satisfy it: a pair multiplies its two sides and a node adds its pairs,
    which is the whole of counting.
